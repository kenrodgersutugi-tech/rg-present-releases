Privacy policy

RG-Present has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no telemetry. Your songs, services, media and settings stay on your own computer, and we never see them.

Last updated 5 November 2026. This policy covers the RG-Present software for Windows and this website.

Who we are

RG-Present is made and published by RG Devs, Kenya. For anything in this policy, including a request about your own data, write to -.

The short version

  • We do not collect anything about you or your church.
  • Your library — songs, services, imported Bibles, pictures, video and settings — is stored only on your computer, in a folder you can open, copy and delete.
  • There are no accounts, no licence keys and no sign-in.
  • There is no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting.
  • Four things can leave your computer, and only in the circumstances set out below: the update check, the web browser you choose to open, the optional AI helpers you choose to turn on, and anything you export yourself.
  • This website sets no cookies and has no trackers.

What RG-Present stores, and where

Everything is kept in one folder on your computer: %APPDATA%\RG-Present\data (Settings opens it for you). It holds:

  • the hymn library, including songs you add or import;
  • your saved services and the order of each one;
  • Bibles that ship with the program and any you import;
  • pictures, video, music and imported presentations;
  • your themes and settings;
  • your Gemini API key, if you choose to add one. It is stored on this computer so the program can use it, and is sent to Google and to nobody else.

None of this is uploaded anywhere. Copying or deleting that folder copies or deletes your data, and uninstalling RG-Present leaves it alone on purpose, so an update never costs you your library.

What leaves your computer

1. The update check

RG-Present asks GitHub whether a newer version has been published, and downloads it if so. The request contains no information about you or your library. GitHub, as the host, will see the usual things any web server sees: your IP address, the time, and which file was asked for. GitHub's own privacy statement governs that.

The check never happens while something is on the audience screen, and it can be turned off in Settings. If you installed RG-Present from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles updating instead and RG-Present makes no update request of its own.

2. The web browser

The browser built into RG-Present only does anything when you open it. Searching uses DuckDuckGo, and any page you then visit is an ordinary visit to that site, which will see what any website sees. The browser keeps its own separate storage, refuses the camera and microphone, and blocks downloads. Nothing it does can reach your library or your service.

3. The optional AI helpers

These do nothing at all until you put a Gemini API key of your own into Settings, and then only when you ask a question. When you do, the request goes directly from your computer to Google (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) and nowhere else. It never passes through us, and we never see it.

What is sent depends on what you asked for:

When you ask for What is sent to Google
A hymn Your description, and a list of the hymns in your library — number, title, any alternative title and folder. Lyrics are not sent.
A reading Your description only. No Bible text is sent.
A service plan The theme you typed, and the same list of hymn numbers, titles and folders.
Notices turned into slides The notes you pasted in.
A generated background The description of the picture you asked for.

Google's handling of that request is governed by Google's own terms for the Gemini API, including what it may do with the content you send, which depends on the kind of Google account and plan the key belongs to. Please read those terms before you turn this on, and do not paste anything confidential — a pastoral note, a member's details — into an AI prompt.

Remove the key in Settings and every one of these features stops at once. Nothing else in RG-Present depends on it.

4. Anything you export yourself

Printing a service, exporting your songs as a pack, or copying your data folder sends that material wherever you send it. That is your decision and your copy.

What we never collect

  • Nothing about the services you run, the songs you sing or the passages you read.
  • No names, addresses, phone numbers or membership records — RG-Present has no place to put them.
  • No usage statistics, no “anonymous” analytics, no crash reports.
  • No device identifiers, licences or machine fingerprints.

If you email us for help, we have whatever you put in that email, and we use it only to answer you.

This website

  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps server logs, which include visitors' IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. We do not have access to them as a report and we do not analyse them.
  • The download and overview pages ask GitHub's public API which version is the latest, so the page can show the version number, date, size and checksum. That request carries nothing about you.
  • There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts, fonts or images. Everything the pages need is served from this site.
  • Whether you chose the light or the dark theme is remembered in your own browser's local storage. It never leaves your browser, and clearing your browsing data removes it.
  • Links to the Microsoft Store and to GitHub take you to those companies' sites, which have their own policies.

Children

RG-Present is a tool for running a service and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anybody, of any age.

Your rights under Kenyan law

The Data Protection Act, 2019 gives you rights over personal data held about you — to be told about it, to see it, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its use. Because RG-Present holds no personal data about its users on any system of ours, in practice there is usually nothing for us to show you or delete. If you have written to us and want that correspondence deleted, or you believe we hold something about you, write to - and we will deal with it within the time the Act allows.

If your church uses RG-Present to show material that includes personal data — a name in a notice, a photograph of a member — your church is the one responsible for that material, not us.

Keeping your library safe

Because everything is on your own computer, its safety is the safety of that computer: keep Windows up to date, use a password on the machine, and copy your data folder to a flash drive from time to time. Only ever download RG-Present from this website or from the Microsoft Store.

Changes to this policy

If RG-Present ever starts doing something this policy does not describe, the policy will be updated here first, with the date at the top changed. We will not add tracking or telemetry quietly.
